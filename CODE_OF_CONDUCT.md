# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project aims to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming place to learn and collaborate. We expect contributors to communicate with patience, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in discussions, reviews, and issue threads.
- Offer constructive feedback with clear technical reasoning.
- Welcome questions from people at different experience levels.
- Respect personal boundaries, time, and differing perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, mocking, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a problem, use GitHub's reporting tools when available or contact the maintainer through the repository profile before escalating publicly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors whose behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.
